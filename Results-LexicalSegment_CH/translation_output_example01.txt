整体译文（准确英文）：
Once upon a time, there was an ancient temple situated on the northern bank of a great river. On the southern bank of the river stood a high platform. Curiously, when asked about its name, everyone called it the Rat Platform. Let me first recount the tale of the Rat Platform as an introduction, and then proceed to tell the strange story of the Catless Kingdom. ... Such is the tale of the Rat Platform. A monk, living in a land with cats, refused to keep one, thereby bringing great calamity upon himself. Now, let me tell another story—one about a little boy who owned a cat. The cat was bought by the king of the Catless Kingdom at a high price, which is indeed a remarkable tale.
