大男得了许多金珠，自然是个大富人。不忍与主人相别，仍在他家里住下。大男却并不恃富而骄，见着主人仍旧是恭恭敬敬；见着同伴，仍旧是和和气气。天天到学堂念书。不消几年，学问就很好了。

整体译文（准确英文）：
The eldest son acquired a great deal of gold and pearls, naturally becoming a man of great wealth. Unable to bear parting with his master, he continued to stay at his home. Yet, he did not grow arrogant with riches; he remained respectful toward his master and amiable toward his companions. Every day, he went to the schoolhouse to study. In just a few years, his scholarship became excellent.
